About Me

I'm a researcher and data scientist with a passion for applying machine learning to scientific problems. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, data engineering, and physical sciences — most recently using neural networks to auto-calibrate imagery from the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO).

I enjoy building reusable, well-tested ML tooling, experimenting with generative models, and contributing to the open-source community. When I'm not training models, you can find me tinkering with my dotfiles or exploring new ideas in Python.
